About TruthScan

TruthScan turns confusing food labels into a simple rating, so you can compare packaged foods in seconds.

How the rating works

Every product starts at 5 and loses points for nutrients that are high per 100 g (or 100 ml for drinks):

NutrientLow (food)High (food)High (drinks)
Sugar≤ 5 g> 22.5 g> 11.25 g
Total fat≤ 3 g> 17.5 g> 8.75 g
Saturated fat≤ 1.5 g> 5 g> 2.5 g
Salt≤ 0.3 g> 1.5 g> 0.75 g
  • High sugar −1.5, high saturated fat −1, high salt −1, high fat −0.5; moderate sugar, saturated fat or salt lose 0.5 each.
  • More than double the "high" limit (e.g. 3 g salt per 100 g) loses another 0.5.
  • Foods above 400 kcal per 100 g lose 0.5; above 500 kcal they lose 1.
  • Drinks: moderate sugar −2, high sugar −2.5, and another −1 above 5 g sugar per 100 ml.
  • Ingredients of concern (e.g. hydrogenated fats, certain colours, preservatives and sweeteners) lose up to 1.5.
  • Fibre and protein add up to 0.75, but not when a product is high in two or more of sugar, saturated fat and salt.
  • The result is rounded to the nearest 0.5, between 1 and 5.
  • If sugar, saturated fat or salt is missing, we show "Not enough data" rather than guess.

Data sources

Product data comes partly from Open Food Facts, a free, collaborative database, made available under the Open Database License. Products you add yourself are saved only on your own device.

Disclaimer

Ratings and warnings are general information based on the label data available to us. They are not medical advice. Labels change and data can contain mistakes, so always read the pack, especially if you have an allergy, and follow your doctor's or dietitian's advice.
