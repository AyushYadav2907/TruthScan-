# TruthScan (GitHub Pages version)

Scan a packaged food's barcode and get a simple 1–5 rating, hidden sugars, additives, allergens
and warnings for your health profile. Everything runs in your browser. Product data comes from
Open Food Facts (ODbL licence), and the rating is worked out on your own device.

Files: five pages (`index`, `product`, `submit`, `profile`, `about` .html), `style.css`, and the scripts
(`common.js`, `engine.js`, `home.js`, `product.js`, `submit.js`, `profile.js`, `html5-qrcode.min.js`).
Keep them all together in the same folder, with `index.html` at the top.

Not medical advice. Have a nutritionist review the rules in `engine.js` before launch.
